### FreezeEverything

First one is the FreezeEverything e2, spawn in on your base (or whatever) and it becomes immovable! Things need to be welded together or something tho (If saves all the props position and angle, and keeps freezing them)

This works best on small to medium size bases. You might have to edit the interval depending on the base size and how fast you want it to update. (Any moving parts in the base will not work)
