## Description

This e2 uses wirelink to hook up an advanced pod controller to the gyropod with only wiring 2 inputs! 1 to the gyro and 1 to the pod controller.  
Don't forget to link the gyropod and the pod controller to the seat or this chip won't work. Yes I realize there is the wireless gyropod but this has different toggleable modes.  
A "flightmod", for when your in space and a "hovermode", for when your on a planet. Very quick and easy to use and it speeds up building a ship.
