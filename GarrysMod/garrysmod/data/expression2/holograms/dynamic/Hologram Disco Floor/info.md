## Hologram Disco Floor

Hi, this is my first ever attempt at making anything with holograms.

Its a simple disco-like floor where all the tiles on the floor change to a random colour.

It's size is also adjustable with the '!size' command in chat (e.g. '!size 4x4').

UPDATE: Now allows fixed colours with new commands '!red', '!green', '!blue' and '!all"
