## Torque Sensor

This simple little device can tell you the torque, speed and power being transmitted across it, by measuring weld deformation.

That means you can put it between an engine and the wheels of a car to tell you how much power is going to the ground in real-time, and in-line.

The dupe is attached, but won't tolerate a 2nd duping, so building you own is preferable. Just spawn the e2 below, then spawn another 075x075 plate and weld and axis their centres together, then adv wire the E2 input of the e2 to the second plate.

For technical specs see the comments at the top of the e2.
