## Description

I played a game called cookie clicker found here: Cookie Clicker
The game is a great way to pass time so I made a makeshift E2 version of it. Enjoy!

### KNOWN BUGS:

- The cookie click sound gets cut off
- Explosion sound gets cut off
- Sometimes the help/pictures do not print
- If you spawn the E2 again on the E2 that has been spawned, the cookie does not go away

### HOW TO PLAY

- Click to bake cookies
- Cursor **(C)** - 10 cookies
- Grandma **(B)** - 50 cookies
- Mine **(M)** - 2000 cookies
- Alchemy Lab **(L)** - 10000 cookies
- Rocket Ship **(R)** - 25000 cookies
- Time Machine **(T)** - 250000 cookies
- Portal **(P)** - 1000000 cookies
- Hadron Collider **(G)** - 1500000 cookies
- Unethical Child Labor **(I)** - 2500000 cookies

**The letters in the () are the keys you need to press in order to buy that item. Each item spawns a hologram.**
