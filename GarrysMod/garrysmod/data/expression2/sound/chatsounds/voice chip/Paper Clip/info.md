## Voice Chip

**Description**
This is a simple entertaining chip which:

1. Uploads a list of all voice sound files to the server
2. Filters and plays these sound files based on your chat in-game

**Controls**
I recommend watching the video to see how to use the chip, but here is a basic list of controls:
E (use): Plays the next sound file in the search result.
Prefix a chat with '.': Keeps the chat silent but voice is still played.

**Video**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mu75gzm2ErY

**Installation**
In the attached .zip there are two files; one is the huge list of voice files, the other is the expression 2. Extract the .zip to \garrysmod\garrysmod\data

Or just place these two files in these locations:
\garrysmod\garrysmod\data\e2files\sounds.txt
\garrysmod\garrysmod\data\Expression2\papers_voice_player.txt

And place the E2 file named 'papers_voice_player' anywhere on the server.
