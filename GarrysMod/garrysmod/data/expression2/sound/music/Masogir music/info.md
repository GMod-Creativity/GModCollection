## Music + applyForce() = Epic Win

Music + applyForce() = Epic Win.

Ok guys, |3ulletje managed to turn the music player into 1 single expression . This is a drastic improvement because you no longer need extra expressions for the clock and for each track being played. Here are some songs to get you started if you would like to make your own music. Just wire "On" to a toggled button:

MAKE SURE TO UPDATE YOUR WIREMOD OR THIS MAY NOT WORK
