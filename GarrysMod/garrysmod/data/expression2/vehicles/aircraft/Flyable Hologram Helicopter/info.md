## Description

This the hologram helicopter I've been working on. It's 100% applyForce/applyAngForce and is designed to be easy and fun to fly rather than being realistic.

### Features:

```plaintext
• Minimalist Design
• Autolanding
• Cannot crash into the ground or ceiling
• Spin up/Spin down guns
• Rotors slow down to a stop when vehicle is exited
• Amazingly nice to fly
• Sounds for Blades, guns and gun motors
• Has fairly low lag so it should be fine on most servers
```

### Controls:

```plaintext
• W/S - Forward/Back
• A/D - Yaw
• Mouse1/Mouse1 - Strafe
• Space/Alt - Ascend/Descend
• R - Fire weapons
• Shift - Speed boost
```
