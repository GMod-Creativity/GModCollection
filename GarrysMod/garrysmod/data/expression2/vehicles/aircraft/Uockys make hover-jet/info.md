**So what are these features you ask?**

- a fully working fuel system /w shootable fuel tank/engine (a damaged tank will make the fuel drain faster and blowing it up will make it go *puff*)
this system was originally intended as a way of having a nice little dog fight battle and should work pretty well to that effect
- pitch controled elevation - meaning no cheapskate hoverballs or anti-gravitational-thinga-ma-bobs
- hover/flight-mode switch
- and last but not least - an interdimentional coffee maker!
please note: "the interdimentional coffee maker X7000" is not actually included due to potential for the destroying the universe by over caffinating unknown alien species

**Controls :**
these should be pretty easy to figure out but i'll post em anyway
F - Switch between hover and flight mode (switch to hover mode in mid air at own risk)
in the dupe there's also a couple of turrets mounted fire 'em with mouse1

- HoverMode:
  - Shift - Forward motion
  - A - Turn Left
  - D - Turn Right *gasp*
- FlightMode:
  - Shift - Forward motion (releaseing shift will cut out the engine and turn off all stabilization)
  - W - Pitch down
  - S - Pitch up
  - A - Turn left
  - D - Turn Right
  - Space - Panic Button for resetting the pitch to 0 incase you're coming in too hot and don't want to smash into the ground

**Known Bugs and requirements**
not too many bugs - slight wobbleing may happen at certain pitch/roll ratios
