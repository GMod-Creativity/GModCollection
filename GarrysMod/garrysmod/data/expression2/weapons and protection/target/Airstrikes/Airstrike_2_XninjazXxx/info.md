AIRSTRIKE

-------------------------------------------------------------------

This E2 spawns a bomb somewhere and after a few seconds the bomb then explodes to reveal that it spawns 3 other bombs and over time those bombs spawn even more bombs causing a total of 9 bombs to fall in that certain area. Right click to use, Use key and reload key to remove all spawned explosives.
