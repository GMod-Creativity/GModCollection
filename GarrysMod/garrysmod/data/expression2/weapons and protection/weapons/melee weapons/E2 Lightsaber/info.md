## E2 Lightsaber. "May the Force Be With You"

E2 Lightsaber
"THE ORIGINAL"

How to use-
-Hold "USE" button to place the light saber in hand.
-"Right Click" to turn the light saber on!
-"Right Click" again to turn off.
-Hold "USE" button again to put the light saber away!
