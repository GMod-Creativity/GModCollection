## iPod v2 [Release]

I decided to re-make my really old iPod that everyone seemed to like. This version uses a GPU instead of Console screen.


(Alternate download available below)

iPod v2 has:
Clean GUI
Built-in cursor
3D intro menu
Pitch slider
Volume slider
All songs from HL1, HL2, EP2, CSS, TF2 and Portal
Custom menu for adding your own music
Hold button for freezing the screen
Multiple-menu switching
