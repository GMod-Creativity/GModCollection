Here is my hyper realistic motorcyckle! it is a singel pistoner but it is quite fast anyways, this I made to be a fun to drive instead of the jeep in HL2, as you can play HL2 through gmod if you own it!!!! ^^
though the constraints do not survive when you are loaded into a new map, you need to spawn a new, but that is OK! 
This realistic means that you as a driver get hurt when enemys shoot at you, I have a tutorial on youtube of how to make a seat that you can take damage in, and this is one of them. The modell of the vechicle is from my "dustblower" bike, and suits the world of Halfelife well I think

Operating the Vehicle:

Enter the drivers seat,
engines start automaticly.
press "W" for throttle,
Steear with "A" and "D"
Gears are manual, gear upp using "mouse1" and down using "mouse2"
The Vehicle have 6 Gears, the 1st is backwards.
Breaks and Clutch is activated by pressing "Space"
Press "Alt" to toggle the headlight

INSTALLATION:

Place the dupe files inside the dupe folder. Any adv dupe tool should work, but I recomend using Advdupelicator 2 because it is the tool with least errors right now. Here is you dupe file folder:

GarrysMod\garrysmod\data\advdupe2\

For me it looks like this:

C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\SteamApps\common\GarrysMod\garrysmod\data\advdupe2\

Paste the txt files from "dupelicator files" folder to the location described above.
