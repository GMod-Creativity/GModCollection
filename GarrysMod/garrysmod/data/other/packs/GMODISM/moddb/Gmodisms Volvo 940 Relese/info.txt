GMODISM DUPELICATION REALESEPARTY 1

This is my Volvo 940 Car. The Volvo is an Swedish Car brand, the name means "we are rolling" in latin. This car is made as close to the original i could do, there are 2 versions in this pack. One regualr diesel version, and one racing petroil version. Both ACF engines are the equivalent to real engines used in the car when it was produced, as the real cars these have backwheel drive! Back wheels are hanged on a suspended bar, frontwheels are individualy suspended. The stearingwheels moves as you turn.

Operating the Vehicle:

Enter the drivers seat,
engines start automaticly.
press "W" for throttle,
Steear with "A" and "D"
Gears are manual, gear upp using "mouse1" and down using "mouse2"
The Vehicle have 6 Gears, the 6th is backwards.
Breaks and Clutch is activated by pressing "Space"
Observe the displays inside the vehicle, they will give you the neccesary information, the vehicle have a fuel tank with high-grade fuel, when the fuel is empty the vechicle will loose full power but work still. Ordinary fuel never runns out because thats how ACF works.

GarrysMod\garrysmod\data\advdupe2\

For me it looks like this:

C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\SteamApps\common\GarrysMod\garrysmod\data\advdupe2\

Paste the .txt files from "dupelicator files" folder to the location described above.
