### Mandera's Hover Board

This is a Hover Board that I've been working on the last few days and I am very proud to finally release it.
It has got alot of nice features such as Jumping, Doing Tricks in air and even a Drifting function.

### Controls

```plaintext
W and S = Forward and Backward movement.
A and D = Turning.
Shift on Ground = Activating Drifting Function.
Space = Jump.
Shift and R in Air = Pitch movement.
Mouse 1 and 2 = Roll movement.
```
