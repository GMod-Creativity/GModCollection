## [Wiremod] Rock Paper Scissor game

This is a wiremod version of the RPS multiplayer game. It uses no E2 chips so is suitable in server who doesn't t accept it.

Players have a controller with 3 buttons to choose what to play in the match.

Buttons:
1 - rock
2 - paper
3 - scissor
Reset choice

Unlink the ropes once deployed.

Let me know if you like it and if you want to add features.
Many thanks for players in Metastruct servers who tested and played the game!
